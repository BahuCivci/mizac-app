1. What the client is
mizac.xyz is a Turkish-language educational site about classical temperament
theory. Once a day it publishes one short educational video, produced from our
own written material, to our own YouTube channel. The API client has no user
interface and no users other than the channel owner: it is a scheduled job.

2. One-time authorization (OAuth) — screenshot 1
The channel owner (safra943@gmail.com) granted access once through Google's
standard consent screen, requesting only the youtube.upload scope. The refresh
token is stored privately. The grant can be revoked at any time from the Google
Account third-party access page, as our privacy policy explains
(https://mizac.xyz/gizlilik, section 8 "YouTube API Servisleri").

3. Daily schedule — screenshot 2
A GitHub Actions workflow runs every day at 07:00 UTC. It takes that day's video
and its title/description and calls the upload function below.

4. The upload — screenshots 3a and 3b
The client refreshes the access token, opens a resumable upload session with the
video's metadata:
    POST https://www.googleapis.com/upload/youtube/v3/videos
         ?uploadType=resumable&part=snippet,status
and then sends the video file to the session URL with PUT.

5. Live run, 11 September 2026 — screenshot 4
For this review we ran the same function once more, uploading a clearly labelled
demonstration video (29.8 MB, 1080x1920, 33 seconds). The call returned:
    video id = q6IZlzMdKnc, uploadStatus = uploaded, privacyStatus = private
Screenshot 4 is the real console output of that run (tokens are not printed).

6. End result — screenshots 5 and 6
The video is on the bahusworld channel in YouTube Studio. Uploads are private
until this review is complete, because videos uploaded from an unverified
project are locked as private; after approval they will be published as public
Shorts.

What the client does NOT do: it does not read, list, modify or delete any
YouTube data; it does not access any other channel or any viewer data; it does
not use YouTube Analytics; and it does not embed or display YouTube content on
our site.

Quota: one upload per day (1,600 units) against the default 10,000. We are not
requesting a quota increase; we submitted the form to complete the compliance
review.
